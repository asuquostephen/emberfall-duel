HOW TO PLAY

Each night you and the keeper play 5 tricks. Play one card each; the higher effective value takes the trick and burns the loser's Ember.

A suit that beats the other gains +3; a beaten suit loses −3. Flame > Thorn > Gale > Tide > Flame.

High Lantern (12) beats anything but costs 2 Ember to light. Banked Ember (0) heals 4. Flare (11) hits +2 when it wins. Wisp (1) steals 3 Ember when it wins.

Win 3 tricks in a row to light a BLAZE — your next trick deals double damage. Play the same suit 3 times running for KINDLING (+4). First to 0 Ember loses; after 7 nights the fuller lantern wins.
